How this system works
  1. Direct generation answers without retrieved context.
  2. Hybrid RRF fuses sparse BM25 and dense retrieval over the configured corpus.
  3. Cross-encoder reranking reorders retrieved candidates for the experimental strong-retrieval mode.
  4. The frozen Luna policy evaluates sequential routing stages; all held-out decisions used RETRIEVE.
  5. Abstention is shown as an explicit policy outcome as a policy or model response.
